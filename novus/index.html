How a shipment moves

Six steps. You see every one.

Each step is recorded in Logi-Sys, so Novus AI and the Track page can show where cargo is, including the Bureau of Customs steps.

Booked

Space confirmed with the carrier.

In transit

Sailing or flying to its destination.

Arrived

Discharged at the port or airport.

Customs

Entry lodged, assessed, duties paid.

Released

Gate pass out, truck dispatched.

Delivered

Signed for at your door.

Common questions

Quick answers.

Novus AI answers these and more, in your language.

When did Novus Freight start?

Novus began in 2017 as JBC Customs Brokerage, founded by two Licensed Customs Brokers and registered with the DTI. It rebranded in September 2020 as Novus Cargo Logistics Inc. and became Novus Freight Inc. in July 2021, registered with the SEC.

Is Novus licensed and accredited?

Yes. Novus lists these local licenses and accreditations: PEZA, Department of Finance, PMTLAI, Civil Aeronautics Board, DOLE, DTI and the National Privacy Commission. Novus Freight Inc. is registered with the SEC.

Which international networks is Novus part of?

Novus is connected to the WCA World, JC Trans and All World Shipping networks, which link it to logistics partners worldwide.

What is Novus’s mission?

To provide customer satisfaction by delivering quality services in a cost-effective and reliable solution resulting in a long-term partnership with our client.

What is Novus’s vision?

The organization envisions one of the most reliable and valued logistics providers, providing clients with the best quality and efficient service possible, with continual development.

What are Novus’s values?

Accountability, Integrity, Quality and Innovation. Novus takes full responsibility for its work, acts ethically, holds itself to high standards and keeps improving to meet clients’ most demanding needs.

Which trade terms does Novus handle?

Novus’s customs clearance covers shipments under CIF, CFR, DDP and DDU.

How many countries does Novus serve?

Novus’s website lists 50+ countries, served through its international networks (WCA World, JC Trans and All World Shipping). For a specific port or route, the team confirms the routing.

Is support available 24/7?

Novus’s website lists 24/7 support. You can also reach the team at (+63) 02 8637-7930 or inquirynovus@nfiph.com.

How do I get a quote?

Send your shipment details through the quote form or email inquirynovus@nfiph.com. Novus replies with the actual rate for your shipment.
