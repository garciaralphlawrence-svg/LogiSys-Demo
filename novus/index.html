Common questions

Quick answers.

Novus AI answers these and more, in your language. Ask it anything about Novus.

Do you handle small shipments?

Yes. Use sea LCL from 1 CBM, or air freight from 45 kg. Many of our clients are SMEs and online sellers importing for the first time.

I’m importing for the first time. Where do I start?

Send us your supplier’s invoice and packing list. We check if your goods need a permit, register you as an importer with BOC if needed, and give you an all-in quote before anything ships.

Can you compute my duties and taxes?

Yes. Our brokers compute duties, VAT and other taxes from your invoice and the tariff code before you pay, so there are no surprises at release.

How long does customs clearance take?

Most complete, green-lane entries clear in 2 to 4 working days after the vessel arrives. Yellow or red lane, missing permits or holds take longer, and we tell you the moment that happens.

What does “assessment” or “lane” mean in customs?

After your entry is lodged, BOC assesses it and assigns a lane. Green lane means no inspection, so it moves straight to duty payment and release. Yellow means a document check; red means a physical exam of the cargo. Novus follows up at every step.

How do I pay?

We bill per shipment by bank transfer. Credit terms are available for regular accounts after review.

Can my cargo be insured?

Yes. We can arrange cargo insurance on request. Ask for it when you request your quote.

Do you ship dangerous goods?

Yes, on request, with the proper safety data sheet (MSDS), packing and declaration. Some classes need carrier approval first, so tell us early.

How do I track my shipment?

Type your Novus tracking number (it starts with NVF-) on the Track page or right here in the chat. You’ll see every step, including the BOC customs steps.

How fast can I get a quote?

You get a ballpark right away on our quote page. A confirmed written quote usually follows within one working day.
