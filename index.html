        Frequently asked questions

        Is Logi-Sys compliant with Philippine customs (BOC)?

        Yes. It is 100% compliant with BOC e2m requirements, ensuring seamless customs workflows.

        Does it support PEZA or Freeport operations?

        Yes. It includes built-in features for PEZA, Freeport, and bonded operations.

        Can small freight companies use Logi-Sys?

        Definitely. The system scales to suit startups, SMEs, and large enterprises alike.

        Do I need to install any hardware?

        No. Logi-Sys is a cloud-based freight management system, so no servers or maintenance are needed.

        What kind of support is available in the Philippines?

        Local support is available, backed by over three decades of global logistics software expertise.

        What is freight forwarding software?

        A software application that streamlines freight operations by letting everyone involved manage end-to-end functions: air freight, sea freight, land freight, warehouse, transport and customs.
